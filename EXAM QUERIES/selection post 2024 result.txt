marks and roll numbers should be updated in the following manner:

In the SCORE file, EQ and roll numbers are available. REGID should be updated from UNVD data on the basis of RKEY (EQ+ROLL). 
Then a new KEY (EQ+REGID) should be generated for updating scores in the APP_DATA.

In the APP_DATA, EQ, REGID and POST_CODE are available but roll numbers and scores are not available. Roll Number should be updated 
from the UNVD data on the basis of PKEY (REGID+POST_CODE) and not on the basis of EQ+REGID. 
This will ensure updating of correct roll numbers. If the roll numbers are updated on the basis of KEY (EQ+REGID), 
incorrect roll numbers will get updated as seen from the above screenshots.
Marks should be updated from the SCORE file on the basis of KEY (EQ+REGID) to ensure 
that scores are correctly updated even if the candidate has applied from more than one regions and
even if different roll numbers are assigned by different regionsfor the same candidate.
